Welcome to Impact Church

This is an evangelical church that offers an alternative celebration for those seeking something more than what a traditional church offers. We believe that God speaks to us through the Bible and that He wants us to live loving Him and others.

Faith

We believe in the transforming power of the Gospel and in God's presence in our daily lives.

Hope

We offer a message of hope and renewal for all who seek deep meaning.

Love

We are a welcoming community where Christ's love is manifested through our actions.

Our Pastor

Guided by faith, united in mission

Giuseppe Capuano

Pastor

Giuseppe Capuano, born in Naples in 1966, was ordained as a Pastor in 2001 in Salerno. In 2011 he moved to Città della Pieve with his wife Julia to start a new work. In 2016 he earned a Degree in Biblical and Theological Sciences from the Theological Faculty of Rome.
