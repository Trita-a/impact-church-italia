Why Give

Your generosity allows Impact Church to continue its mission: sharing the Gospel, supporting the community and making a difference in people's lives. Every gift, large or small, has an eternal impact.

"Each one must give as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."

— 2 Corinthians 9:7

How Donations are Used

Transparency is a fundamental value for us.

Ministry and Teaching

Support for pastors, educational materials, and biblical training for the community.

Church Expenses

Rent, utilities, maintenance and everything needed to maintain a welcoming space.

Social Work

Help for families in difficulty, community projects and charitable initiatives.

Missions

Support for missionaries and evangelistic projects in Italy and the world.
