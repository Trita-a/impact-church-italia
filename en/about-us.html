Our History

Impact Church Italia was born in 2011 in Città della Pieve, in the province of Perugia. We are an evangelical church that believes in the transforming power of the Gospel. Our desire is for people to be impacted and transformed by the truth of Christ.

Our Beliefs

The values and beliefs that guide our faith

This is an evangelical church that offers an alternative celebration for those seeking something more than what a traditional church offers. We believe that God speaks to us through the Bible and that He wants us to live loving Him and others.

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the Word of God, inspired and infallible, the supreme guide for our faith and conduct.

Jesus Christ

We believe in Jesus Christ as the Son of God, who died for our sins and rose for our salvation.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the Holy Spirit who dwells in every believer, guides, comforts, and gives power to live a holy life.
