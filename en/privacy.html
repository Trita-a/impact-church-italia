Privacy & Cookie Policy

Transparency, security, and protection of your personal data.

Last Updated: January 30, 2026

Welcome to the Impact Church Italia website. Your privacy is fundamental to us. This policy is drafted pursuant to Art. 13 of EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR) and transparently explains how we manage your information.

The data controller is Impact Church Italia.

  • Address: Via Gino Bombagli, snc - 06062 Città della Pieve (PG), Italy

2. Types of Data Collected

While navigating and interacting with our site, we may collect the following categories of data:

  • Navigation Data: IT systems automatically acquire certain data (e.g., IP address, browser type, access times) whose transmission is implicit in the use of Internet protocols.
  • Data Voluntarily Provided: Optional sending of emails or filling out forms (e.g., "Contact Us") involves the acquisition of your address and other data entered to respond to requests.

3. Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed exclusively to:

  • Ensure the correct technical functioning of the site.
  • Respond to information, prayer, or contact requests sent by users.
  • Anonymous statistical analysis to improve our services (via technical/analytical cookies).

4. Cookie Policy

This site uses cookies to improve the browsing experience.

  • Technical Cookies: Necessary for the functioning of the site (e.g., language preferences).
  • Analytical Cookies: We use tools like Google Analytics in anonymized form to understand how the site is used.

We do not use profiling cookies for advertising purposes. You can disable cookies at any time from your browser settings.

5. Rights of the Data Subject

In accordance with GDPR, you have the right to ask the Controller for:

  • Access to your personal data.
  • Rectification or deletion of the same ("right to be forgotten").
  • Limitation of processing or opposition to it.
  • Data portability.

To exercise your rights, you can send a simple request via email to the address indicated above.
